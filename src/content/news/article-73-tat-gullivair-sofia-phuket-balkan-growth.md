The Tourism Authority of Thailand (TAT) reported two Balkan-market activities in a [release of 9 November 2026](https://www.tatnews.org/2026/11/tat-targets-balkan-market-growth-through-new-air-access-and-travel-trade-partnerships/).

## Flights

- GullivAir's seasonal **Sofia–Phuket** service runs **once weekly** on a 240-seat Airbus A330-200.
- The inaugural flight GBG701 left Sofia on 5 November and was due in Phuket on 6 November.
- The Bulgarian airline also plans a Sofia–Bangkok route.

## Trade

- The TAT Prague x Unite for Travel Agent FAM Trip runs **2–10 November** with 12 Romanian operators, in partnership with flydubai.
- It covers Krabi, Phuket and Phang-nga, including the Phi Phi and Similan Islands.
- Business meetings with 15 Thai tourism businesses each were set for 4 November (Krabi) and 9 November (Phuket).

## Numbers

From 1 January to 31 October 2026, Thailand received 6,266,567 visitors from Europe. Of these, 97,655 came from the Balkans, up 5.42% from last year. Bulgaria sent 15,029 (up 8.07%).

## Why it is on the desk

This is a Europe watch-list item about Balkan air access and a Romanian trade trip. The release does not mention India, fares or any change in Thai entry rules.
