*Checked again on 15 November 2026 (IST). Verified is false: a search still did not find a Korea Tourism Organization or Korean government page for these roadshows or for a 21% rise in Indian arrivals. Everything below rests on one trade-press report. Treat it as a lead, not a confirmed notice.*

The Korea Tourism Organization (KTO) New Delhi office held its Korea Tourism Roadshow 2026 in **Ahmedabad on 28 October** and **Mumbai on 4 November**, according to [ETTravelWorld](https://travel.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/events/domestic/korea-tourism-organization-conducts-roadshows-in-ahmedabad-and-mumbai-reports-21-rise-in-indian-arrivals/135628834) (7 November 2026).

## Reported points

- **Arrivals:** KTO said Indian visitor arrivals to Korea rose **21%** in January–September 2026. The report gives no absolute number.
- **Support programmes for Indian agents:** the MICE Support Program 2026, the Edu Tourism Support Program 2026–2027 and the Korea Group Visa facility.
- **Flights:** Director Yoonmi Cho cited daily Delhi–Seoul flights by Korean Air and Air India. Korean Air presented its daily Delhi–Seoul service at the event.
- **Jeju:** KTO showcased the upcoming film *Jeju Olle*, featuring actress Anushka Sen, to introduce Jeju to Indian audiences.
- **Segments covered:** leisure, MICE, education, culture, luxury, wellness and weddings, plus K-culture, K-beauty and food.

## Reader note

For Northeast India travellers drawn to Korean pop culture, visa rules and fees matter most; see our South Korea visa guide. Nothing in the report changes those rules.

## What we could not confirm

The ETTravelWorld piece does not give the 21% base figures, the terms of the group-visa facility, or the film's release date.
