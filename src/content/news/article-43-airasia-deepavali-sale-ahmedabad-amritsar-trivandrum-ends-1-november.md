*Based on an AirAsia press release dated 26 October 2026. Checked on 1 November 2026 (IST). Time-sensitive: the booking window closes today.*

AirAsia is running **Deepavali special fares** on routes that include three Indian cities: **Ahmedabad, Amritsar and Trivandrum**. The release also lists Langkawi, Bali, the Maldives, Seoul and Melbourne. The airline did not publish fare amounts. It says the fares include passenger service charges, fuel surcharges and other fees, and are valid on selected flights only.

## Key dates

- **Booking period:** 26 October to **1 November 2026**
- **Travel period, Ahmedabad:** from 25 October 2026. The English release says until 30 September 2027; the Malay version of the same release says 31 August 2027.
- **Travel period, Amritsar:** 2 December 2026 to 30 September 2027
- **Travel period, other routes:** 2 November 2026 to 30 September 2027

## Extras

- A **one-time change fee waiver** (fare differences may apply).
- **Up to 30% off Value Packs** (checked bags, meals, seats), only when bought during the first booking on the AirAsia MOVE app, subject to availability.

Bookings are through the AirAsia MOVE app and airasia.com.

## Note for agents

Amritsar travel starts on 2 December, which matches the restart date AirAsia gave for its Kuala Lumpur–Amritsar flights (see our [routes tracker](https://tangvah.github.io/travel-news-india/routes-tracker/)). Because the two versions of the release give different end dates for Ahmedabad travel, check the fare rules at booking.
