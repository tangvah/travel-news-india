*Based on an AirAsia press release dated 1 November 2026 (Manama). Checked on 2 November 2026 (IST).*

AirAsia has appointed the **board of directors of AirAsia Bahrain**, the airline group said in a [release from Manama on 1 November](https://newsroom.airasia.com/news/airasia-reaffirms-long-term-commitment-to-bahrain-appoints-airasia-bahrain-board). The group wants Bahrain to be its **hub linking Asia with the Middle East, Europe and Australia**.

## Key points

- **Flights:** AirAsia says it is **targeting the start of AirAsia flights to Bahrain by March 2027**, "as conditions allow". Tony Fernandes, CEO of Capital A and adviser to AirAsia Group, said the situation in the region had forced the group to adjust its first plans, but that its commitment to Bahrain had not changed.
- **Board:** Dr Serra Kirdar chairs the board, with Dato' Fam Lee Ee as vice chairman. The other members are Dr Faisal Khalid Kanoo, Nawaf Al Zayani, Akram Safa, Lord Ahmad of Wimbledon and Tan Sri Jamaludin Ibrahim. All are non-executive directors.
- **Background:** the release says the move follows a letter of intent signed between Capital A and Bahrain's Ministry of Transportation and Telecommunications in November 2025, and AirAsia's February 2026 announcement naming Bahrain as its Middle East hub. AirAsia also says it has received Bahrain's Golden License.
- **Cargo and engineering:** Capital A's logistics arm Teleport has run twice-weekly freighter flights into Bahrain since February 2026 and is moving to larger freighters. Its engineering unit, Asia Digital Engineering, plans to build maintenance (MRO) capability in Bahrain.

## Why it matters for India

The release does not mention India or name any routes. AirAsia flies to India from Kuala Lumpur and is [bringing back its Ahmedabad and Amritsar routes](https://newsroom.airasia.com/news/airasia-celebrates-return-of-ahmedabad-and-amritsar-flights-with-a-special-sale) this season. A Bahrain hub would give the group a Gulf base between Asia and Europe. We will watch for route announcements that include India once flying begins.
