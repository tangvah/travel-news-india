*Update to our 25 October report, which covered 1 to 22 October. Based on the Sri Lanka Tourism Development Authority (SLTDA) weekly report for 1 to 31 October 2026. Checked on 3 November 2026 (IST).*

SLTDA's report for the full month of October shows that Sri Lanka received **168,625 tourists**, up **2.1%** on the 165,193 it received in October 2025. India stayed far ahead as the largest source market, with **62,211 arrivals, or 37%** of the total ([SLTDA weekly report, 1 to 31 October 2026](https://www.sltda.gov.lk/storage/common_media/weeklyReportOctober1-31.pdf)).

## Key points

- **October's top five markets:** India 62,211 (37%), the United Kingdom 12,402 (7%), China 11,190 (7%), Australia 8,509 (5%) and Germany 8,027 (5%). Arrivals from India were more than those from the UK and China combined.
- **Last nine days of October:** [our earlier report](/travel-news-india/news/article-20-sri-lanka-india-38-percent-arrivals-october-2026/) counted 45,530 Indian arrivals from 1 to 22 October, so about 16,700 more Indians arrived between 23 and 31 October.
- **Year to date:** from 1 January to 31 October 2026, Sri Lanka received **1,862,304** tourists. India supplied **499,625** of them, about 27%. The next markets were the UK (173,129), China (123,740) and Germany (105,305).
- **Overall trend:** October was the first month since May with growth on the year before. September was almost flat (−0.3%). By our calculation from the monthly figures in the same report, arrivals for January to October were about 1.5% below the same ten months of 2025 (1,890,687).

## Why it matters for India

India sends more than a third of Sri Lanka's visitors as the island moves into its main winter season. India needed fewer than 400 more arrivals after 31 October to pass 500,000 for the year, a milestone likely to come in the first days of November. With India so dominant, Indian agents are in a strong position when they negotiate with Sri Lankan hotel and ground partners for the winter season. As before, clients should apply for their Sri Lanka ETA only on the official government portal.
