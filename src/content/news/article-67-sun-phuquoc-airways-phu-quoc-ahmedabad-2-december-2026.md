*Checked on 9 November 2026 (IST). Verified is set to false because the flight numbers and timings come from state media (VOV), not the airline's own page.*

Sun PhuQuoc Airways, a Vietnamese airline, will connect Vietnam's Phu Quoc island with Ahmedabad on a nonstop route from **2 December 2026**. It is the airline's first route to India.

## What the airline says

On its [offer page](https://www.sunphuquocairways.com/gl/en/explore/offers/direct-flights-phu-quoc-ahmedabad-20-percent-off), Sun PhuQuoc Airways says bookings opened on 16 October 2026. The first flight is scheduled for 2 December 2026, with **five flights a week on Monday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday and Sunday**. It gives a flight time of about 5 hours 25 minutes.

## More detail from VOV (not on the airline's page)

[VOV](https://english.vov.vn/en/travel/sun-phuquoc-airways-to-launch-first-nonstop-service-to-india-post1342084.vov), citing Sun Group, reports these further details:

- Flight 9G315 leaves Phu Quoc at 7:35 pm and reaches Ahmedabad at 11:30 pm. Flight 9G314 leaves Ahmedabad at 1:00 am and lands in Phu Quoc at 7:15 am (local times).
- The aircraft is an Airbus A321neo with a full-service offering. Meals will not use beef or pork; vegetarian and Halal options are available on request.
- The airline plans to move to seven flights a week from early February 2027.
- Phu Quoc had about 150,000 Indian visitors in 2025 (up 328%), and Vietnam received 746,000 Indian arrivals, making India its sixth-largest source market.

## Why it matters

Ahmedabad already has Vietnam Airlines' Hanoi flights, which began on 3 November (see our earlier report). This new route gives Gujarat travellers a direct beach-island option as well. We have not seen the airline's regulatory approvals, so the schedule should be treated as subject to change until the first flight operates.
