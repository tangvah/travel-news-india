*Based on Sun PhuQuoc Airways' offer page. Checked on 9 November 2026 (IST).*

Sun PhuQuoc Airways, a Vietnamese airline, will connect Vietnam's Phu Quoc island with Ahmedabad on a nonstop route from **2 December 2026**. It is the airline's first route to India.

## What the airline says

On its [offer page](https://www.sunphuquocairways.com/gl/en/explore/offers/direct-flights-phu-quoc-ahmedabad-20-percent-off), Sun PhuQuoc Airways says bookings opened on 16 October 2026. The first flight is scheduled for 2 December 2026, with **five flights a week on Monday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday and Sunday**. It gives a flight time of about 5 hours 25 minutes.

## Why it matters

Ahmedabad already has Vietnam Airlines' Hanoi flights, which began on 3 November (see our earlier report). This new route gives Gujarat travellers a direct beach-island option as well. We have not seen the airline's regulatory approvals, so the schedule should be treated as subject to change until the first flight operates.
