*Based on official Air India Express and Air India customer-support replies on X on 6 November 2026 (IST). Checked on 7 November 2026 (IST). This updates our earlier Saudi suspension notes ([article 30](/travel-news-india/news/article-30-indigo-saudi-arabia-flights-cancelled-until-14-november/), [article 41](/travel-news-india/news/article-41-air-india-express-saudi-flights-suspended-until-14-november/) and [article 44](/travel-news-india/news/article-44-akasa-jeddah-suspension-extended-18-november-saudi-flights-update/)) without replacing them.*

Indian carriers’ Saudi Arabia suspensions are still in force ahead of mid-November. Fresh **customer-support replies** on the airlines’ official X accounts — not new formal blanket notices — are the wording travellers are being given now. We have not seen a new standalone #TravelAdvisory post that extends every carrier beyond the dates already reported.

## What Air India Express is telling passengers

On 6 November (01:45 IST), [@AirIndiaX](https://x.com/AirIndiaX/status/2118437047589638396) told passengers that, due to the prevailing situation, flights to and from **Jeddah (JED), Riyadh (RUH) and Dammam (DMM)** “will remain suspended until **14 November 2026 (inclusive)**.” An [earlier reply the same morning (01:40 IST)](https://x.com/AirIndiaX/status/2118435831883223518) used the same wording.

That matches the desk’s earlier read of the Air India / Air India Express advisory covered in [article 41](/travel-news-india/news/article-41-air-india-express-saudi-flights-suspended-until-14-november/).

## What Air India is telling passengers

On 6 November (02:58 IST), [@airindia](https://x.com/airindia/status/2118455520793374996) replied that, in view of the evolving situation in Saudi Arabia, all Air India flights to and from **Riyadh and Dammam** “remain cancelled **until further notice**,” adding that the airline is monitoring developments to resume safe operations. A [later reply the same day (22:07 IST)](https://x.com/airindia/status/2118744705123168277) used the same “until further notice” language for Riyadh and Dammam (including when asked about Jeddah–Mumbai restart timing).

## Akasa and IndiGo

We did not find a new Akasa #TravelUpdate after the 1 November note that kept **Jeddah** suspended through **18 November** and **Riyadh** through **30 November** ([article 44](/travel-news-india/news/article-44-akasa-jeddah-suspension-extended-18-november-saudi-flights-update/)). IndiGo’s earlier public line of cancellations through **14 November** for Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam and Madinah ([article 30](/travel-news-india/news/article-30-indigo-saudi-arabia-flights-cancelled-until-14-november/)) was not replaced by a newer formal advisory in the sources checked this morning.

## What we could not confirm

- Whether Air India’s “until further notice” for Riyadh/Dammam formally supersedes the 14 November date in the 30 October advisory, or is softer customer-service language for the same window.
- A new IndiGo or Akasa advisory dated after 1 November.
- Exact refund/rebooking windows for tickets issued after the last published advisories — use each airline’s app, contact centre or city office.

## Why it matters for Indian travellers and agents

Umrah, family and labour traffic to Jeddah, Riyadh, Dammam and Madinah remains disrupted. **Do not assume a 15 November restart** for every airline or city: Air India Express still cites **14 November inclusive**; Air India is telling some travellers Riyadh/Dammam are **until further notice**; Akasa’s last formal note runs longer on Jeddah and Riyadh. Reconfirm 48 hours before travel and offer Gulf alternatives (UAE, Bahrain, Oman, Qatar) only where schedules are actually operating.
