*Based on a Tourism Authority of Thailand press release dated 16 November 2026. Checked on 17 November 2026 (IST).*

The Tourism Authority of Thailand (TAT) is partnering with ICONSIAM and other public and private organisations to stage **Amazing Thailand Countdown 2027** from **27 to 31 December 2026** at **River Park, ICONSIAM, Bangkok**. [TAT's release](https://www.tatnews.org/2026/11/tat-and-iconsiam-unveil-amazing-thailand-countdown-2027-in-bangkok/) says the event will be broadcast live and covered by international media.

## What is planned

- **Fireworks:** a **1,400-metre** display above the Chao Phraya on New Year's Eve, synchronised with lighting and music. TAT says the fireworks are environmentally friendly and made from Thai sticky rice.
- **Performers:** more than **200** Thai and international performers across the five days.
- **Around the site:** regional Thai food, contemporary cultural performances and riverside activities at River Park.

ICONSIAM's managing director, Supoj Chaiwatsirikul, said it will be ICONSIAM's largest countdown to date, after eight years of hosting New Year events on the river.

## Why it matters for the trade

TAT links the event to its "Value over Volume" strategy and its Festival Economy approach, which targets higher-spending international visitors. It says it wants Bangkok's Chao Phraya to rank among the world's top five countdown destinations, and encourages visitors to extend stays to other Thai destinations. For Indian agents selling Bangkok New Year packages, this gives a dated, free-to-watch event to build around. The release gives no ticketing, entry or seating details, so check with ICONSIAM before quoting them to clients.
