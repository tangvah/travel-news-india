*Based on the Tourism Authority of Thailand (TAT) November event calendar, published 29 October 2026. Checked on 30 October 2026 (IST).*

TAT has published its event calendar for November 2026. For Indian travellers, the two Pattaya events are the easiest to add to a short Bangkok trip. Several longer-running shows also last into the winter peak season.

## Pattaya (Chon Buri)

- **Pattaya International Jazz Festival 2026:** 6–7 November, on Pattaya Beach.
- **Pattaya International Fireworks Festival 2026:** 27–28 November, on Pattaya Beach.

## Long-running events for winter trips

- **Udon Thani International Horticultural Expo 2026:** 1 November 2026 to 14 March 2027, at Nong Dae Wetland in Udon Thani. The expo features plants, flowers, landscape design and sustainable horticulture.
- **Nasatta Light Festival 2027:** 20 November 2026 to 28 February 2027, at Nasatta Thai Park in Ratchaburi. It is a night-time light show.

## Culture, music and sport elsewhere

- **Amazing Nan Marathon 2026:** 1 November, through historic Nan town.
- **BottomsUp Music Festival 2:** 1 November, Chao Lao Beach, Chanthaburi.
- **Park Utopia x Chiang Rai Fest:** 6–7 November, Singha Park Chiang Rai.
- **Neramit Sakon Nakhon 2026:** 6–15 November, 18:00–22:00, Sakon Nakhon Old Town.
- **Nakhon Nayok Winter Festival and Fantasy Kite Festival 2026:** 11–15 November, Khun Dan Prakan Chon Dam.
- **Sisaket Coffee & Music Fest 4:** 13–15 November, Si Sa Ket.
- **Miracle of Faith at Phanom Rung Festival 2026:** 13–15 November, Phanom Rung Historical Park, Buriram.
- **Phrae OTOP and Red Cross Fair 2026:** 28 November to 8 December, Phrae.

## Tip for agents

The Bangkok Art Biennale also runs until 28 February 2027 (see our earlier report). With the Pattaya festivals, it gives a Bangkok–Pattaya package a dated hook for November. Check exact programmes and timings with the organisers closer to the date.
