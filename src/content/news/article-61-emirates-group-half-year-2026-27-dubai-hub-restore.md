*Based on the Emirates Group half-year results release dated 5 November 2026 and Visit Dubai’s travel-updates page (last updated 30 October 2026). Checked on 7 November 2026 (IST).*

The Emirates Group [reported](https://www.emirates.com/media-centre/emirates-group-announces-half-year-profit-for-2026-27-despite-full-six-month-impact-of-regional-conflict-and-high-fuel-costs/) a **profit before tax of AED 4.1 billion** (US$ 1.1 billion) for the first six months of financial year 2026-27, **down 66%** from the same period last year. Group revenue was **AED 66.2 billion** (US$ 18.0 billion), down 12%. The release attributes the pressure to regional conflict disruption and high fuel costs.

## What travellers should take from the release

Chairman and Chief Executive Sheikh Ahmed bin Saeed Al Maktoum thanked the UAE government for coordinating the safe resumption of air transport and said government measures had led to the **easing of travel advisories to and through the UAE**, allowing Emirates to **restore operations with confidence**.

Operational markers stated in the release:

- At **30 September 2026**, Emirates was operating **91%** of its pre-disruption network.
- Passenger and cargo network: **150 airports** in **78** countries and territories.
- Passengers carried **1 April–30 September**: **21.8 million**, down 22% year on year; average seat factor **72.0%** (versus 79.5% a year earlier).
- Capacity (ASKM) down 17%; passenger traffic (RPKM) down 25%.
- Cash position on 30 September: **AED 54.1 billion** (US$ 14.7 billion).

Emirates says it used free date changes, refund policies and an advisory-unrestricted travel cover to support customers during the period. Fleet and product investment continued, including nine new A350 deliveries and cabin refurbishments.

Separately, [Visit Dubai’s travel updates](https://www.visitdubai.com/en/travel-advisory) page (last updated **30 October 2026**) states Dubai is open to visitors, with hotels, attractions, restaurants, retail and transport operating, and asks travellers to check official UAE government, media and airline channels.

## What we could not confirm

The half-year release does not break out India-route frequencies, India passenger numbers, or a city-by-city restoration list for Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru or other Indian gateways. It also does not replace day-of-travel flight status. Saudi Arabia flight suspensions by Indian carriers remain a separate story (see our earlier desk notes on IndiGo, Air India, Air India Express and Akasa).

## Why it matters for Indian travellers and agents

Dubai remains a major holiday and transit hub for Indians. The official message is that **UAE advisories have eased and Emirates has largely restored its Dubai-hub network**, while profits and loads are still below last year’s record. Agents should still verify individual India–Dubai flights and any third-country advisories before ticketing, and prefer emirates.com / visitdubai.com over unofficial “advisory” forwards.
