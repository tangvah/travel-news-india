*Checked on 9 November 2026 (IST). Verified is false: we read the English version on the Vietnam.vn government portal; we did not open the Ministry's original notice or the attached document.*

The Vietnam National Tourism Administration (VNAT) plans a programme to introduce Vietnamese tourism and connect businesses in **Mumbai and Bengaluru** from **6 to 13 December 2026**, according to a notice dated 12 October 2026 on the [Vietnam.vn portal](https://www.vietnam.vn/en/moi-tham-gia-chuong-trinh-xuc-tien-du-lich-viet-nam-tai-an-do).

## What the notice says

- The notice is an invitation to **Vietnamese businesses**, asking them to take part in promoting Vietnamese tourism in India. It cites a decision of the Minister of Culture, Sports and Tourism dated 10 September 2026 on growth in the tourism industry.
- The programme includes a business-to-business (B2B) exchange, a tourism promotion session and a networking event.
- Participating firms are exempt from event organisation costs, but pay their own travel, accommodation and personal expenses. They are asked to prepare products suited to Indian tastes and to contribute to a prize draw for Indian businesses attending.
- Businesses had to register by **25 October 2026**, a deadline that has now passed.
- The notice says official participation dates will be confirmed to businesses once legal procedures are complete. So the 6–13 December dates are "scheduled", not final.

## What we could not confirm

The notice does not give venues, the split of days between the two cities, or how Indian agents can register. We have no confirmation yet from VNAT or the Vietnamese mission in India. Indian agents should wait for an official invitation.

## Context

Vietnam has become a bigger India market this autumn, with new direct flights (see Vietnam Airlines Ahmedabad–Hanoi and IndiGo Mumbai–Hanoi).
