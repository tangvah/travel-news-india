*Based on posts from the official X accounts of Akasa Air, Air India Express, Air India and IndiGo. Checked on 2 November 2026 (IST).*

Akasa Air has extended its Saudi Arabia suspension again. In a [travel update posted on 1 November](https://x.com/AkasaAir/status/2116817612365803633) (14:30 IST), the airline said that, because of the security situation in the Kingdom, its flights to and from:

- **Jeddah** are suspended **until and including 18 November 2026**, and
- **Riyadh** are suspended **until and including 30 November 2026**.

Akasa's [previous update, on 23 October](https://x.com/AkasaAir/status/2113656951360139468), had suspended Jeddah until 10 November, so the Jeddah date has moved by eight days. The Riyadh date is unchanged.

## Refunds and rebooking on Akasa

Akasa says affected passengers can choose a **full refund**, which it will credit to the original payment method **within seven days**, or **reschedule at no extra cost**. Passengers who booked through a travel partner should contact that partner.

## Where the other Indian airlines stand

- **Air India and Air India Express:** their [joint advisory of 30 October](https://x.com/airindia/status/2116140470518870472) suspends flights to and from Jeddah, Riyadh and Dammam until 14 November 2026 (inclusive). ([as we reported earlier](/travel-news-india/news/article-41-air-india-express-saudi-flights-suspended-until-14-november/)) In a [customer reply posted early on 2 November (IST)](https://x.com/AirIndiaX/status/2116973447951810873), Air India Express added that its flights **from 15 November 2026 onward are scheduled to operate as planned**.
- **IndiGo:** in a [customer reply on 31 October (IST)](https://x.com/IndiGo6E/status/2116283469924069751), IndiGo said its flights to and from Madinah, Jeddah, Dammam and Riyadh stand cancelled until 14 November, citing airport restrictions.

## What to do

- **Travellers on Akasa:** Jeddah tickets up to 18 November and Riyadh tickets up to 30 November will not operate. Choose a refund or a free date change.
- **Agents:** Akasa's Jeddah restart is now four days later than the 14 November date used by Air India, Air India Express and IndiGo. Check each airline's latest notice before rebooking Umrah or work travellers, as all of these dates have been extended more than once.
