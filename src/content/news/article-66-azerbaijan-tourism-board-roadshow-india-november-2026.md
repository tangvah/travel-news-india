*Based on the Azerbaijan Tourism Board event page for its India roadshow (listed dates 16–20 November 2026). Checked on 8 November 2026 (IST).*

The Azerbaijan Tourism Board (ATB) [plans a trade roadshow in India](https://www.tourismboard.az/event/446-roadshow-in-india) from **16 to 20 November 2026**, with stops in three cities:

- **16 November 2026** — Chennai
- **18 November 2026** — Ahmedabad
- **20 November 2026** — New Delhi

The official event page says the roadshow will present Azerbaijan’s leisure and business tourism opportunities, introduce leading Azerbaijani tourism companies to Indian industry partners, and hold bilateral meetings between travel companies from the two countries.

## What the page tells participating companies

ATB lists the contact as Nariman Zeynalli, with the email **rovshan.rahimov@tourismboard.az**. The participation fee is given as **1,000 AZN**, excluding VAT. Travel expenses, the page says, are covered by the participating company.

Registration on the page “is not a confirmation of attendance.” Participation is considered after re-confirmation at the end of the registration process, and companies that have confirmed attendance “will receive an e-mail with a detailed agenda and locations of events.” The **registration deadline was 5 October 2026**, which has now passed.

## What we could not confirm

The event page does not name the hotels or venues in Chennai, Ahmedabad or New Delhi, list the Azerbaijani companies due to attend, or say whether Indian agencies that missed the 5 October deadline can still join the meetings. It also does not give current India–Azerbaijan arrival figures, flight schedules or visa rules. A separate azerbaijan.travel press note about earlier India roadshows is dated **24 January 2023** and is not a source for this 2026 programme.

## Why it matters for Indian travellers and agents

For agents in Chennai, Ahmedabad and Delhi who already work — or want to work — with the Caucasus, the next two weeks are when ATB says Azerbaijani partners will be in India for product presentations and B2B meetings. Agencies that confirmed attendance should watch for the promised agenda email. Others looking for details will need to use the contact published on the [ATB event page](https://www.tourismboard.az/event/446-roadshow-in-india) rather than assume walk-in access.
