*Based on Noi Bai International Airport's official English news page and IndiGo's 2 September 2026 press release. Checked on 6 November 2026 (IST).*

IndiGo's direct **Mumbai–Hanoi** service is now flying. [Noi Bai International Airport](https://www.noibaiairport.vn/en/noi-bai-international-airport-congratulates-indigo-airlines-on-launch-of-new-hanoi-mumbai-route-nid21351.html) has published an official note congratulating IndiGo on the launch, and describes a ceremony at the terminal for flight **6E1638** bound for Mumbai, including cake-cutting with airport and airline representatives.

IndiGo [announced](https://www.goindigo.in/press-releases/indigo-strengthens-connectivity-to-hanoi.html) the route on 2 September 2026 as a **daily** service effective **25 October 2026**, complementing its existing daily Hanoi flights from Delhi and Kolkata. The airline said **IndiGoStretch** would be offered on the sector.

## Schedule (from IndiGo's release; local times)

| Sector | Flight | Days | Departure | Arrival |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mumbai–Hanoi | 6E 1637 | Daily | 04:15 | 10:00 |
| Hanoi–Mumbai | 6E 1638 | Daily | 19:05 | 23:05 |

IndiGo noted the schedule was subject to regulatory approvals.

## What we could not confirm

Noi Bai's page does not state the calendar date of the inaugural ceremony. IndiGo's own press index (as of 1 November) still shows the September announcement rather than a separate inaugural release. Aircraft type is not stated in the IndiGo release we opened. Always reconfirm times on goindigo.in or in your GDS before quoting.

## Why it matters

Western India now has a daily nonstop to Hanoi alongside Akasa Air's existing Mumbai–Hanoi service. For agents, it is another Vietnam option from Mumbai without a Delhi or Kolkata connection. Indian travellers still need a Vietnam visa or e-visa before travel; use only the government's official e-visa portal.
