*Based on a Singapore Tourism Board media release dated 27 October 2026. Checked on 6 November 2026 (IST).*

Visa and Marina Bay precinct partners — **Marina Bay Sands**, **UOB** and the **Singapore Tourism Board (STB)** — have [launched 'A Moment to Savour'](https://www.stb.gov.sg/about-stb/media-publications/media-centre/visa-and-marina-bay-precinct-partners---marina-bay-sands--uob-and-singapore-tourism-board---present--a-moment-to-savour---bringing-international-restaurant-collaborations-to-singapore-/), a premium dining programme of limited-time restaurant collaborations in the Marina Bay area from **November 2026 to March 2027**, under the Visa Destinations platform.

STB says the series aims to showcase Singapore as a global gastronomy hub for visitors and locals. Experiences are offered to **Visa cardholders** globally, with pre-sales for UOB Visa cardholders and Visa Infinite cardholders.

## Collaboration calendar (from STB)

| Period | Collaboration |
| --- | --- |
| November 2026 | Waku Ghin (Marina Bay Sands) with MOSU Seoul, Chef Anh Sung-Jae |
| December 2026 | Jin Ting Wan with Jing Xi Hui; Labyrinth with **Masque (Chef Varun Totlani)**; plus a Black Pearl multi-chef collaboration |
| January 2027 | Saint Pierre with Taian Table |
| February 2027 | Peach Blossoms with Nusara and pastry chef Dej Kewkacha |
| March 2027 | JAAN by Kirk Westaway with NOBUO; Odette with L'air du Temps; 1887 by André (visiting chef TBC) |

Bookings for November and December sessions opened from **28 October 2026**, with timed UOB and Visa Infinite presales before general Visa sales. All times in the release are **Singapore time (SGT)**. Reservations are directed to [destinations.visa.com/singapore](https://destinations.visa.com/singapore). Seats are limited and subject to availability. Details for some 2027 chef pairings will be announced later, STB says.

## What we could not confirm

The release does not publish menu prices, exact dinner dates within each month (beyond the November "two-night" debut note for Waku Ghin), or whether Indian-issued Visa cards beyond Infinite receive the same privileges as UOB regional cards. Confirm live availability and pricing on the Visa Destinations page before selling.

## Why it matters for Indian travellers and agents

December's **Labyrinth × Masque** pairing puts Mumbai chef **Varun Totlani** on the Marina Bay calendar — a clear India angle for food-led Singapore trips. Affluent Indian Visa / Visa Infinite holders planning Singapore between November and March can watch the Visa Destinations booking window; agents should sell it as a **limited culinary add-on**, not a mass package, and pair it with Marina Bay hotels and attractions already in the itinerary.
