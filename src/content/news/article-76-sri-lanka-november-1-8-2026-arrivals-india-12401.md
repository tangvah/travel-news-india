*Update to [our 8 November report](/travel-news-india/news/article-63-sri-lanka-november-1-5-2026-arrivals-india-6259/). Based on the Sri Lanka Tourism Development Authority (SLTDA) weekly report for 1 to 8 November 2026. Checked on 11 November 2026 (IST).*

SLTDA's [weekly report](https://www.sltda.gov.lk/storage/common_media/Weekly_Report_1st_to_8th_November_2026.pdf) counts **49,929** tourist arrivals in Sri Lanka from 1 to 8 November 2026, and **1,912,233** for 1 January to 8 November.

## Key points

- **India:** 12,401 arrivals, **24.8%** of the period total, the largest market. Year to date India has **512,026**.
- **Other top markets, 1–8 November:** Russian Federation 5,483 (11.0%), United Kingdom 3,541 (7.0%), Germany 3,449 (7.0%), China 2,586 (5.0%), Australia 2,324 (5.0%).
- **Year to date, after India:** United Kingdom 176,670, China 126,326, Germany 108,754, Australia 97,822.
- **Daily range:** the report's chart shows roughly 5,300 to 7,700 arrivals a day in the period.
- **November so far:** 49,929, against 212,906 for the full month of November 2025. The month is incomplete, so the two are not comparable yet.

## What we could not confirm

The report gives no breakdown of Indian arrivals by airport or purpose, and no November forecast.

## Reader note

India remains Sri Lanka's biggest market through the winter season. Apply for a Sri Lanka ETA only on the official government portal.
