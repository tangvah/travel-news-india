*Update to [our 3 November report on October arrivals](/travel-news-india/news/article-48-sri-lanka-october-2026-arrivals-india-62211-update/). Based on the Sri Lanka Tourism Development Authority (SLTDA) weekly report for 1 to 5 November 2026. Checked on 8 November 2026 (IST).*

India has now sent more than **500,000** visitors to Sri Lanka in 2026. SLTDA’s [weekly report for 1 to 5 November](https://www.sltda.gov.lk/storage/common_media/Weekly_Report_1st_to_5th_November_2026.pdf) puts India’s year-to-date total at **505,884** through 5 November — up from the **499,625** recorded to 31 October in the [previous weekly report (1–31 October)](https://www.sltda.gov.lk/storage/common_media/weeklyReportOctober1-31.pdf).

In the first five days of November, Sri Lanka received **28,633** tourists. India remained the largest source market, with **6,259 arrivals, or 22.0%** of that total.

## Key points from the 1–5 November report

- **Daily totals:** 5,623 on 1 November, 5,450 on 2 November, 5,734 on 3 November, 5,364 on 4 November and 6,462 on 5 November, adding up to 28,633.
- **Top markets, 1–5 November:** India 6,259 (22%), the Russian Federation 3,178 (11%), the United Kingdom 2,145 (8%), Germany 1,978 (7%), China 1,552 (5%), Australia 1,447 (5%), France 1,391 (5%), the Maldives 741 (3%), the United States 641 (2%) and Spain 599 (2%). Other markets supplied 8,702 arrivals (30%).
- **Year to date, 1 January to 5 November 2026:** Sri Lanka received **1,890,937** tourists. After India (505,884), the next markets in SLTDA’s top-twenty table were the United Kingdom (175,274), China (125,292), Germany (107,283), Australia (96,945), the Russian Federation (92,919) and France (85,293). India’s 505,884 arrivals are about **27%** of the year-to-date total.
- **October context:** the same report restates October 2026 at **168,625** arrivals, up **2.08%** on October 2025 (165,193), matching the full-month figures we reported on 3 November.

## What we could not confirm

The weekly report does not say on which calendar day India’s 2026 total crossed 500,000, and it does not break the 6,259 Indian arrivals by airport, purpose of visit or average spend. It also gives no full-month forecast for November 2026 (the November cell shows only the 28,633 counted so far). We have not treated secondary press summaries of these figures as sources.

## Why it matters for Indian travellers and agents

India is still Sri Lanka’s dominant winter-season market, now past a round 500,000-arrival mark for 2026, with a **22%** share even in a five-day window that is more mixed than October’s **37%** India share. That keeps Indian agents in a strong position when they negotiate hotel and ground allotments for the rest of the peak season. As before, clients should apply for a Sri Lanka ETA only on the official government portal.
