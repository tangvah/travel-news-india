Two long-haul routes we have been tracking are now flying: **SWISS** between **Bengaluru and Zurich**, and **Air India** between **Mumbai and Toronto**.

## SWISS: Bengaluru–Zurich

SWISS said on X that flight **LX140** "took off for Bengaluru for the first time" on the afternoon of 27 October, making Bengaluru its **third Indian destination**. SWISS CEO Jens Fehlinger opened the route with a ribbon-cutting ceremony at Zurich Airport.

SWISS has said the route runs five times a week, and that Bengaluru stays in its summer 2027 schedule (see our earlier report). For trade sellers, the route gives south Indian travellers a one-stop option to Switzerland and onward connections across the SWISS and Lufthansa Group networks.

## Air India: Mumbai–Toronto

Toronto Pearson Airport posted that Air India's new non-stop **Toronto–Mumbai** service is "officially open", and that it celebrated the launch with Air India, partners and community members. The airport said it is Air India's **second route from Toronto**, operated with a **Boeing 777-300ER**, and that it serves the largest diaspora corridor between India and Canada.

When Air India announced the route, it described a seasonal service of three flights a week for the winter, running until 26 March 2027. Check Air India's website for current timings.

## What to check before booking

- Confirm visa and entry requirements for Switzerland and Canada with the official embassy or immigration website before booking.
- See our [routes tracker](https://tangvah.github.io/travel-news-india/routes-tracker/) for other new international flights from India this winter.
