Air India has introduced a **new guest amenities collection** on international flights. Its newsroom lists the release as "Air India unveils new guest amenities inspired by India, designed for the world", dated 27 October 2026.

## What's in the collection

According to Air India's post on X, the collection brings together:

- **collectable amenity kits** featuring original artwork by **Gond artist Dwarka Paraste**;
- **Forest Essentials** skincare;
- the airline's own **Ayurvedic Marma Massager** and other wellness items; and
- **Kullu Dawn** loungewear.

Air India says the items are inspired by India's cultural heritage and are "now available onboard select/applicable international flights". It did not say in the post which routes or cabins get each item.

## Why it matters for the trade

This follows Air India's 22 October announcement of a new fine-dining service in business class on long-haul international flights. Both announcements concern Air India's onboard product on international flights. Agents selling premium cabins can use these details when comparing Air India with foreign carriers, but should confirm which flights and cabins carry the new kits.
