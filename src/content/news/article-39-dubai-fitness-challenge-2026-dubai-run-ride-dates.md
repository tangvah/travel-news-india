*Based on the Government of Dubai Media Office and the official Dubai Fitness Challenge website. Checked on 31 October 2026 (IST).*

The **10th Dubai Fitness Challenge (DFC)** begins today and runs **until 29 November 2026**. It asks residents and visitors to do 30 minutes of physical activity every day for 30 days. The Dubai Department of Economy and Tourism and the Dubai Sports Council organise the challenge. According to the Government of Dubai Media Office, the 2026 edition includes four flagship events (Dubai Run, Dubai Ride, Dubai Stand Up Paddle and Dubai Yoga) along with Fitness Villages, Fitness Hubs and thousands of classes and activities across the city.

## Key dates

| Event | Date | Start time (Dubai) | Details |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dubai Ride | Sunday 1 November 2026 | 06:00 | Mass cycling event; the Media Office says it has brought cyclists together on Sheikh Zayed Road since it was added to the programme. 2026 route details were not on the official page when checked |
| Dubai Run | Sunday 22 November 2026 | 06:30 | 5 km route to Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid Boulevard in Downtown Dubai, or 10 km route along Sheikh Zayed Road to the Gate Building in DIFC; both start near the Museum of the Future |

Dubai is 1 hour 30 minutes behind IST.

## Scale

The Media Office says DFC has drawn more than 16 million participants since it began in 2017, and more than three million people took part in a single edition by 2025. Dubai Run was first held in 2019, Dubai Ride was added the following year, Dubai Stand Up Paddle in Hatta followed in 2023 and Dubai Yoga in 2024. The number of Fitness Hubs grew from 11 in 2019 to 30 in 2025.

## Why it matters for Indian travellers

- **Short trips:** with both main events on Sunday mornings, a long-weekend trip to Dubai from India can include Dubai Ride on 1 November or Dubai Run on 22 November.
- **For the travel trade:** the November window suits corporate wellness groups, running clubs and family packages. Check the official site, dubaifitnesschallenge.com, for registration and participation rules before selling event-linked packages.
