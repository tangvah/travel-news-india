*Update to [our 1 November preview](/travel-news-india/news/article-42-phuket-global-wellness-summit-2026-tat-healing-new-luxury/). Based on a Tourism Authority of Thailand (TAT) press release dated 11 November 2026. Checked on 12 November 2026 (IST).*

Her Majesty Queen Suthida presided over the opening of the **20th Global Wellness Summit (GWS) 2026** at Angsana Laguna Phuket on 11 November, according to [TAT](https://www.tatnews.org/2026/11/her-majesty-the-queen-of-thailand-opens-global-wellness-summit-2026-in-phuket/). The summit runs from 10 to 13 November.

## Key points

- **Organisers:** Thailand's Department of Thai Traditional and Alternative Medicine (DTAM), with TAT, the Thailand Convention and Exhibition Bureau (TCEB) and Phuket Province as co-hosts.
- **Attendance:** around 600 delegates, including wellness leaders, investors and medical experts.
- **TAT showcase:** TAT's Governor presented Thailand's wellness tourism experiences across the country's five regions to Her Majesty during an exhibition tour.
- **Programme:** the summit covers the global wellness economy, longevity, preventive healthcare, hospitality and innovation. The Global Wellness Institute presented its 2026 Global Wellness Economy Monitor on the opening day.

## What it means for the trade

TAT is using the summit to promote Thailand as a wellness destination. Indian agents who sell wellness, spa or medical-wellness packages can follow TAT's newsroom for any product announcements after the summit closes on 13 November.

## What we could not confirm

The release does not announce new wellness packages, India-specific campaigns or incentives.
