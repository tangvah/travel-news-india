*Based on an Emirates release of 29 October 2026. Checked on 30 October 2026 (IST).*

Emirates Skywards is running a winter promotion with its non-airline partners. Skywards is the loyalty programme of Emirates and flydubai. Members can earn extra Miles on hotels, holiday packages and car rentals booked from **29 October to 30 November 2026**.

## The offers

- **Emirates Skywards Hotels:** 50% bonus Miles on hotel stays.
- **Emirates Holidays:** 50% bonus Miles on holiday packages.
- **Booking.com:** double Miles on hotel stays.
- **IHG Hotels & Resorts:** double Miles on eligible stays.
- **ALL Accor:** 25% bonus Miles when members convert ALL Accor Reward points into Skywards Miles.
- **Car rentals:** double Miles with SIXT, Europcar, Avis, Budget and Hertz.

## Dates to note

- **Hotels and car rentals:** book between 29 October and 30 November 2026. The stay or rental must be completed by 31 January 2027.
- **Emirates Holidays packages:** book between 29 October and **26 November 2026**, for travel until 31 January 2027.

## Why it matters for Indian travellers

The travel window runs to 31 January 2027, so it covers the December-January holiday period. Indian Skywards members who book hotels or a rental car for a winter trip through these partners can earn more Miles than usual. Members should check the partner terms at emirates.com before booking, because the release describes some hotel stays as "eligible" without giving details.
