*Based on the Tourism Authority of Thailand (TAT) release of 10 November 2026. Checked on 11 November 2026 (IST).*

TAT [says](https://www.tatnews.org/2026/11/tat-welcomes-record-michelin-guide-thailand-2027-selection-marking-10th-edition/) the MICHELIN Guide Thailand 2027, its 10th edition, covers a record **507** dining establishments, including **53 starred restaurants**.

## Key facts from the release

- **Breakdown:** 2 Three-Star, 10 Two-Star and 41 One-Star restaurants, plus 151 Bib Gourmand and 303 MICHELIN-Selected venues.
- **New this year:** three promotions to Two Stars, 10 additions to One Star, 26 new Bib Gourmand and 40 new Selected entries.
- **Chiang Mai:** Belén by Paulo Airaudo becomes the city's first starred restaurant.
- **Phuket:** PRU is the first restaurant outside Bangkok and its surrounding provinces to earn Two Stars. Omemo and Jaras join the One-Star list.
- **Bangkok:** Sorn and Sühring keep Three Stars; Blue by Alain Ducasse and Mia are promoted to Two Stars.
- **TAT view:** the governor said gastronomy is central to the "Healing is the New Luxury" campaign.

## Reader note

For Indian travellers, the release points to Chiang Mai and Phuket as dining destinations beyond Bangkok. It gives no India-specific offers, prices or booking details, so check each restaurant directly for reservations. The full selection is on the official MICHELIN Guide website.
