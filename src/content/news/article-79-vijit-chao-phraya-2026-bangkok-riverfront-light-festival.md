*Based on a Tourism Authority of Thailand press release dated 13 November 2026. Checked on 15 November 2026 (IST).*

The Tourism Authority of Thailand is running the fifth **Vijit Chao Phraya** from **13 November to 13 December 2026**. [TAT's release](https://www.tatnews.org/2026/11/vijit-chao-phraya-2026-brings-a-month-long-spectacle-of-light-and-imagination-to-bangkoks-riverfront/) places the festival at **19 riverfront locations** between Rama VIII Bridge and Rama III Bridge, plus a floating light installation on the river. Entry is **free**, and the displays run **18:00 to 22:00 every day**.

The theme is **"The Light of Imagination"**. The programme uses light and sound, projection mapping, lasers, drone formations, fireworks and cultural performances. New this year, TAT is working with Chulalongkorn University, Thammasat University and King Mongkut's Institute of Technology Ladkrabang on some of the installations.

## Shows with fixed dates

Times below are as TAT published them. Other sites have their own nightly light-and-sound slots, so do not treat this list as the whole programme.

- **Rama VIII Bridge:** six five-minute laser shows each night, at 19:00, 19:30, 20:00, 20:30, 21:00 and 21:30. Waterfall-style fireworks at **19:30 on 13 and 15 November**, and at **20:45 on 20 and 27 November and 4 and 11 December**.
- **Wat Arun:** **800-drone** shows at **19:50** on 13, 15, 18, 20, 25 and 27 November, and on 2, 4, 9 and 11 December.
- **Memorial Bridge (Phra Phutthayotfa Bridge):** fireworks at **20:45** on 13–15, 21–22 and 28–29 November, and on 5–6 and 12–13 December.
- **Rama III Bridge:** fireworks at **21:20 on 13 and 15 November**, and at **20:45 on 20 and 27 November and 4 and 11 December**.
- **"Vessel of Joy & Smiles":** a lit barge between the Sathorn area and Rama III Bridge on **13–15 November**, from 18:00 to 22:00.

TAT says show times and special performances differ by place and date. People can watch from the bank or book a sightseeing or dining cruise with a licensed operator.

## Why it matters for the trade

TAT ties the festival to its night-economy work: more evening activity, and more demand for cruises, hotels, restaurants and riverside transport. For agents selling Bangkok in the high season, it is a free evening add-on, but fireworks and drone nights are not every day. Quote a show only for the dates above, or check the Vijit Chao Phraya 2026 Facebook page or www.thailandfestival.org, which are the contacts TAT gives.
