*Based on a Government of Dubai Media Office release dated 1 November 2026. Checked on 4 November 2026 (IST).*

Sheikh Hamdan bin Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum, Crown Prince of Dubai, has [announced two new tourism measures](https://mediaoffice.ae/en/news/2026/novemebr/01-11/hamdan-bin-mohammed). Both are due to be rolled out **during November 2026**.

## 1. Tourist visa applications on Visit Dubai

Visit Dubai, the city's official tourism website, will accept applications for **single-entry and multiple-entry tourist visas**, and more visa categories are planned later. The Dubai Media Office says **GDRFA Dubai** (the General Directorate of Identity and Foreigners Affairs) will still assess applications and issue visas.

The move is part of Dubai's Shared Digital Channels Initiative, which puts government services on a small number of shared platforms. The aim is to let visitors plan a trip, book attractions and apply for a visa in one place instead of using several websites and apps.

## 2. Dubai Access Club

Dubai Access Club is a membership programme for **repeat visitors**, open **by invitation only**. The government calls it the world's first city-led tourism membership programme. It says invited members will get exclusive experiences, services and benefits meant to make return trips easier and more rewarding.

## What is not known yet

The official release does not give visa fees, processing times, eligibility rules or an exact launch date for the Visit Dubai service. It also does not say how Dubai Access Club invitations will be issued, what the benefits are, or whether membership has a cost. The Media Office says more service details will be announced "in due course". Some media reports give a membership fee and visa terms for the club, but we could not find these in the official release, so we have not included them.

## What it means for Indian travellers and agents

- **Nothing changes yet.** Until the new service is live, keep applying through the channels you use now.
- **Use only official sites.** When the Visit Dubai option opens, check that you are on visitdubai.com and be wary of look-alike sites. GDRFA Dubai remains the issuing authority.
- **Frequent visitors**, such as business travellers, families visiting relatives and repeat holidaymakers, are the people Dubai Access Club is aimed at. Watch for details of who qualifies.

We will update this story when Dubai publishes the service details.
