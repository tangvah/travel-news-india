*Based on IndiGo's official press releases, rechecked on 22 October 2026.*

The northern winter schedule starts on 25 October. In its first three days, IndiGo is due to open three new international routes from India, according to its press releases.

## Mumbai–Hanoi: daily from 25 October
| Flight | Days | Departs | Arrives |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 6E 1637 Mumbai–Hanoi | Daily | 04:15 | 10:00 |
| 6E 1638 Hanoi–Mumbai | Daily | 19:05 | 23:05 |

The route will offer IndiGoStretch, the airline's business product. IndiGo says this adds to its existing daily Delhi–Hanoi and Kolkata–Hanoi flights. Times are local.

## Gaya–Bangkok: twice weekly from 26 October
IndiGo's press index lists non-stop, twice-weekly Gaya–Bangkok flights from 26 October. The airline presents the route as a link between Bodh Gaya's Buddhist pilgrimage circuit and Thailand. The index entry does not include flight numbers or timings.

## Guwahati–Bangkok: from 27 October
This will be IndiGo's first international route from Guwahati. IndiGo says it also opens direct international operations from Northeast India.
- **Inaugural, Tuesday 27 October:** 6E 1086 Bangkok 08:50 – Guwahati 10:25; 6E 1085 Guwahati 18:15 – Bangkok 22:45.
- **From 31 October, Wednesdays and Saturdays:** the same flight numbers and times.

## Why it matters
- **For travellers:** Travellers in Bihar and the Northeast will no longer need to connect through Kolkata or Delhi to reach Bangkok. Mumbai gets a daily non-stop to Hanoi.
- **For agents and trade:** The Gaya flights suit pilgrimage groups from Thailand, and Hanoi suits business travel. IndiGo's releases say all schedules are **subject to regulatory approvals**, so check that flights are operating before ticketing groups.
- **Entry reminder:** Thailand's 30-day visa exemption for Indians and Vietnam's e-visa rules are covered in our visa tracker.
