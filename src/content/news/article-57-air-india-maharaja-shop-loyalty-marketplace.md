*Based on an Air India newsroom press release dated 5 November 2026. Checked on 6 November 2026 (IST).*

Air India has [launched The Maharaja Shop](https://www.airindia.com/in/en/newsroom/press-release/air-india-maharaja-club-launches-the-maharaja-shop.html), a premium lifestyle marketplace for **Maharaja Club** members. The airline says members can earn **Maharaja Points** on eligible purchases across curated Indian and global brands, then redeem those points toward **Award Flights** and **cabin upgrades** on Air India.

## What the shop offers

- Categories listed in the release include electronics, luggage, travel accessories, fragrances, home essentials, wellness, watches and jewellery.
- Members get **complimentary shipping on eligible orders**.
- An **Air India exclusives** merchandise range — magnets, aircraft-inspired keychains, lapel pins, postcard and coaster sets and travel essentials — is sold only on The Maharaja Shop.
- The shop is **members only**. Non-members must enrol in Maharaja Club before buying and earning.

Sunil Suresh, Air India's Head of Loyalty, E-Commerce and Marketing, said the shop extends Maharaja Club beyond flights so members can build points through everyday spending.

## Context Air India gives for the programme

Today, Maharaja Club members can earn points with Air India, Air India Express and more than **100** partners across airlines, hospitality, retail, lifestyle and financial services, and redeem on Air India, Air India Express and **25** Star Alliance partner airlines. Members also have access to more than **1,100** lounges worldwide, according to the release.

## What we could not confirm

The release does not publish earn rates, a full brand list, a shop URL path beyond the newsroom story, or whether points from merchandise stack with other promotions. Treat earn/redeem maths as unpublished until Air India posts them on the shop or in programme terms.

## Why it matters for Indian travellers and agents

Frequent Air India flyers who already hold Maharaja Club membership can add non-air spend toward the same Award Flight and upgrade pool. Agents advising high-value clients can flag enrolment before a shopping or gift season, but should not invent earn rates. The product is India-centric lifestyle loyalty rather than a destination promotion.
