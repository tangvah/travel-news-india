*Based on the Immigration Department of Malaysia, the High Commission of Malaysia in New Delhi and Tourism Malaysia. Checked on 31 October 2026 (IST).*

Two months remain on Malaysia's visa-free entry scheme for Indian passport holders. As of 31 October 2026, the **Immigration Department of Malaysia** still lists Indian citizens as visa-exempt **until 31 December 2026**, and the **High Commission of Malaysia in New Delhi** gives the same end date. Neither page mentions an extension into 2027.

## What the rules say now

According to the High Commission, Indians can enter Malaysia without a visa for **up to 30 days** for business, tourism, social visits or transit until 31 December 2026. Visitors must have:

1. A passport valid for at least six months;
2. A confirmed return flight ticket;
3. A confirmed hotel booking; and
4. A **Malaysia Digital Arrival Card (MDAC)**, submitted online within three days before arrival.

The MDAC is filed at the Immigration Department's online portal (imigresen-online.imi.gov.my/mdac). It is required even under the visa waiver.

## What it means for 2027 trips

No official notice of an extension had been published when we checked. Unless Malaysia announces one, Indian travellers arriving from 1 January 2027 will need a visa. Malaysia's eVISA is applied for at malaysiavisa.imi.gov.my.

Tourism Malaysia has said its Visit Malaysia campaign has been **extended through 2027** and that it will step up promotion in India. In a 12 August 2026 release, it said Malaysia received more than 1.5 million visitor arrivals from India in 2025 and that India is served by more than 190 direct flights a week. The campaign extension is not a visa decision, however. Only the Malaysian government's notice on the visa waiver itself will settle the 2027 position.

## Advice for travellers and agents

- **Trips that start on or before 31 December 2026** are covered by the current waiver, for up to 30 days.
- **For January 2027 and later**, check the Immigration Department page before travel. Where possible, choose flights and hotels that can be changed until the position is confirmed.
- **Late-night departures on 31 December:** the official pages describe the waiver as covering travel to Malaysia until 31 December 2026. A flight that lands after midnight arrives in 2027, so confirm with the High Commission before relying on the waiver.

We will update this story when Malaysia announces its decision.
