*Based on posts by the official X accounts of Guwahati International Airport, Vizag Airport, IndiGo, Air India and Air India Express on 27 October 2026, and IndiGo's 7 September 2026 announcement. Checked on 28 October 2026 (IST).*

Two more Indian cities now have non-stop flights to **Bangkok**. IndiGo's **Guwahati–Bangkok** service and Air India Express's **Visakhapatnam–Bangkok** service both started on **27 October 2026**.

## Guwahati–Bangkok (IndiGo)

- Guwahati International Airport said on X that non-stop flights to Bangkok with IndiGo start "today", operating **every Wednesday and Saturday**.
- In a second post, the airport described the inauguration: a ceremonial lamp lighting, a boarding pass handover and celebrations with airport officials and airline staff.
- When IndiGo announced the route on 7 September, it said the service marked the start of its international operations from Guwahati and of direct international operations from Northeast India.

## Visakhapatnam–Bangkok (Air India Express)

- Vizag Airport (Alluri Sitarama Raju International Airport) posted that it celebrated the launch of Air India Express's non-stop Visakhapatnam–Bangkok service with the airline, airport teams and the first passengers.
- Air India Express thanked Civil Aviation Minister Ram Mohan Naidu, who had welcomed the new route on X, and said it is proud to serve Visakhapatnam.
- Air India Express has not published the schedule or aircraft for this route in the posts we checked, so confirm timings on its website or your booking system.

## Weather warning for Visakhapatnam

Air India Express, Air India and IndiGo all issued travel advisories on 27 October saying **Tropical Cyclone Arnab** may affect flights to and from Visakhapatnam and Vijayawada. Air India said operations may also be affected on **28 October**. IndiGo said affected customers are being told through the contact details given at booking, and may choose a refund or an alternative flight if their flight is cancelled. Agents with clients flying via Visakhapatnam this week should check flight status before departure.

## Why it matters

The Guwahati link gives travellers from Assam and the wider Northeast a direct route to Thailand without connecting through Kolkata or Delhi. Visakhapatnam's link does the same for north coastal Andhra Pradesh.
