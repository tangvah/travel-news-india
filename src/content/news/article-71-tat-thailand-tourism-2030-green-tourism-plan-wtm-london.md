*Checked against the TAT release on 10 November 2026 (IST).*

The Tourism Authority of Thailand (TAT) presented its long-term sustainability approach at World Travel Market (WTM) London 2026. TAT Governor Thapanee Kiatphaibool spoke on The Wanderlust Headliner Stage at Excel London on 4 November, according to the [TAT Newsroom release of 5 November 2026](https://www.tatnews.org/2026/11/tat-presents-thailand-tourism-2030-sustainability-vision-on-wanderlust-headliner-stage-at-wtm-london/).

## What TAT said

- **Goal:** to become Asia's No. 1 High-Value and Meaningful Destination by 2027, under its "Value Over Volume" direction and the "Healing is the New Luxury" theme.
- **Spreading visitors:** TAT is promoting **55 target tourism communities** and **19 priority destinations** across five regions. Its target is a **60:40 split** of visitors between major and lesser-known destinations.
- **Green standards:** under the Thailand Green Tourism Plan 2030, TAT is promoting sustainability standards for operators, including TAT STGs STAR and CF Hotels.
- **Model destinations:** Krabi is a prototype for green tourism, and the approach is planned to extend to a Chiang Mai prototype.
- **Partners:** TAT says its Transform Together initiative involves 11 ministries, industry associations and private-sector partners.

## Why it matters for Indian trade

Thailand is a core market for Indian agents. The release does not mention India or any visa or fee change. It does show where TAT wants to steer itineraries: longer stays and lesser-known destinations beyond the usual Bangkok, Phuket and Pattaya circuit. Agents building multi-city packages may find more TAT support for those routes.

## What we could not confirm

The release gives no dates for the Chiang Mai prototype and no figures for the current visitor split.
