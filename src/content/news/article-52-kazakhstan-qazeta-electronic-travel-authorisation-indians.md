*Based on a notice from Kazakhstan's embassy in the Netherlands, published on gov.kz on 2 November 2026, and the Kazakh foreign ministry's 2022 notice on visa-free entry for Indians. Checked on 15 November 2026 (IST).*

Kazakhstan is introducing **QazETA**, a free electronic travel authorisation for foreign nationals. A [notice on the Kazakh government's website](https://www.gov.kz/org/mfa-hague/press/news/details/1322393?lang=en), from the embassy in the Netherlands, says the government adopted the resolution on **10 October 2026**. The permit does not replace a visa where a visa is required, does not lengthen the allowed stay, and does not guarantee entry.

## When the checks start

- **From 1 November 2026:** Aktau International Airport and four road crossings — Urlyutobe (Pavlodar region), Aukhatty (Zhambyl region), Atameken (Turkistan region) and Nurly Zhol (Zhetysu region). This phase is already under way.
- **From 1 January 2027:** Astana and Almaty international airports.
- **From 1 March 2027:** all other border checkpoints.
- **From 1 July 2027:** QazETA is **mandatory**. Without it, entry can be refused.

Until 1 July 2027, not having a QazETA is not, by itself, a reason to refuse entry. Officers are to tell travellers that they need one.

## Indians' stay limit

The [Kazakh foreign ministry](https://www.gov.kz/org/mfa/press/news/details/398648?lang=en) says Government Decree 464 of 7 July 2022 lets Indian citizens stay visa-free for up to **14 days at a time**, and no more than **42 days in any 180 days**. QazETA does not extend that stay.

## How to apply

Apply on the [QazETA site](https://qazeta.kz/en) or in the official app **at least 72 hours** before the planned border crossing. The authorisation is **free**, valid for **180 days**, and allows more than one entry in that period. The same notice says it can also be obtained **at the border**.

The notice's exemption list is short: holders of diplomatic or service passports, members of official delegations, accredited diplomats, and people who already have a valid Kazakh visa or residence permit. It does not publish a longer list of age-based exemptions.

## What agents should do

For trips into Almaty or Astana before 1 January 2027, the airport check has not started. From January, add QazETA to the checklist and apply at least three days ahead, even though a missing permit is not itself grounds for refusal until 1 July 2027. The 14-day visa-free limit for Indians still applies.
