*Based on posts from Ahmedabad Airport's official X account on 31 October and 3 November 2026, and a Vietnam Airlines notice dated 13 October. Checked on 4 November 2026 (IST).*

Vietnam Airlines has started flying between **Ahmedabad and Hanoi**. In a [post on 3 November](https://x.com/ahmairport/status/2117634704426119439), Ahmedabad Airport welcomed the airline and said the launch was marked with a traditional lamp-lighting ceremony, a cake-cutting and the handover of the first boarding pass.

A [Vietnam Airlines notice dated 13 October](https://spirit.vietnamairlines.com/bay-cao-khat-vong-cong-hien/kham-pha-quyen-loi/super-low-budget-cung-vietnam-airlines-kham-pha-thanh-pho-di-san-ahmedabad.html) set 3 November as the start date, and the airport's [31 October post](https://x.com/ahmairport/status/2116500978660978948) repeated it.

## The route at a glance

- **Frequency:** four times a week, according to both the airport and the airline.
- **Aircraft:** Airbus A321, according to the Vietnam Airlines notice.
- **Network:** Vietnam Airlines says Ahmedabad is its fifth Indian city, after Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru and Hyderabad. It now has seven routes between India and Vietnam, with 25 round trips a week, from Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City.

## What we could not confirm

Neither source gives flight numbers, operating days or departure times. Check the current schedule on vietnamairlines.com or in your GDS before quoting. We will update our [routes tracker](https://tangvah.github.io/travel-news-india/routes-tracker/) if the airline publishes these details.

## Why it matters

- **For Gujarat travellers:** this is a direct, full-service option to northern Vietnam from their home airport, without connecting through Mumbai or Delhi.
- **For agents:** remind clients to apply for Vietnam e-visas only on the government's [official e-visa portal](https://evisa.immigration.gov.vn/en_US/web/guest/khai-thi-thuc-dien-tu/cap-thi-thuc-dien-tu). The portal's application page showed a "temporarily unavailable" notice when we checked on 4 November.
