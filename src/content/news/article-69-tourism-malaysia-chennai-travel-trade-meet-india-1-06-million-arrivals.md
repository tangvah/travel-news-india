Tourism Malaysia Chennai and the Consulate General of Malaysia in Chennai held an airlines and travel trade meeting on **5 November 2026** at the Taj Coromandel, Chennai, according to the release text published by [CNN O TV](https://cnnotv.com/55224-2/) and [Greater Chennai Today](https://greaterchennaitoday.com/tourism-malaysia-chennai-strengthens-industry-partnerships-to-boost-malaysia-india-tourism/).

## Numbers in the release

- Indian visitors to Malaysia: about **1.13 million in 2024**, **1.56 million in 2025**, and about **1.06 million from January to September 2026**.
- South India is estimated to account for about **60%** of Indian visitor traffic.
- Chennai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad and Kochi are named as regional gateways.

## What was discussed

The release says participants discussed higher airfares, currency swings, changing spending patterns, regional competition and geopolitical uncertainty as challenges. Tourism Malaysia outlined its Visit Malaysia 2026–2027 priorities (destination marketing, airline cooperation, trade engagement, media outreach and consumer promotions) and talked about itineraries beyond Kuala Lumpur and longer stays. Air connectivity was a key topic, including keeping existing South India–Malaysia flights and exploring more routes. The Consul General, Aida Safura Niza Othman, called for closer cooperation with the trade.

## Visa reminder

The Immigration Department's [country list](https://www.imi.gov.my/index.php/en/main-services/visa/visa-requirement-by-country/) still says Indian citizens are visa-exempt **until 31 December 2026**. We have not found any extension (see our earlier report). The release does not mention the visa question.

## Why it matters

Malaysia's reported 2026 Indian arrivals for nine months already sit at roughly two-thirds of the 2025 full-year total. Agents booking Malaysia for travel after 31 December should confirm the visa position before selling.
