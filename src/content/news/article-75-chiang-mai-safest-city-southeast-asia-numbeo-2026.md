*Based on the Tourism Authority of Thailand (TAT) release of 10 November 2026. Checked on 11 November 2026 (IST).*

TAT [welcomes](https://www.tatnews.org/2026/11/chiang-mai-ranked-safest-city-in-southeast-asia-and-19th-worldwide-in-2026/) Chiang Mai's placing as the safest city in Southeast Asia and 19th worldwide in Numbeo's Safety Index by City 2026, with a score of **77.9**.

## Key facts from the release

- **What is measured:** confidence walking alone by day and night, worries about theft and violent crime, and perceived overall crime levels.
- **Caveat:** per TAT, Numbeo compiles the index from user-contributed surveys. It reflects perceptions of safety, not official crime statistics.
- **Other recognition:** Chiang Mai ranked tenth in Best Cities at the Travel + Leisure Luxury Awards Asia Pacific 2026, and Doi Inthanon National Park was named in the Green Destinations Top 100 Stories 2026 ([see our earlier report](/travel-news-india/news/article-72-doi-inthanon-green-destinations-top-100-stories-2026/)).

## Reader note

Indian ordinary passport holders can visit Thailand visa-free for up to 30 days for tourism, under the scheme in force since 15 September 2026 ([Royal Thai Embassy, New Delhi](https://newdelhi.thaiembassy.org/en/content/the-revision-of-thailand-s-visa-exemption-and-voa)). The ranking is a survey-based perception score and TAT's release has no India-specific offers or flight information.
