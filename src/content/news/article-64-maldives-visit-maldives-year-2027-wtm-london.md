*Based on a Visit Maldives news release dated 3 November 2026. Checked on 8 November 2026 (IST).*

The Maldives has [unveiled Visit Maldives Year 2027](https://visitmaldives.com/en/news/maldives-unveils-visit-maldives-year-2027-at-wtm-london) — branded **“Celebrate the Sunny Side”** — to international media and travel-trade partners at World Travel Market London 2026. Visit Maldives says President Dr Mohamed Muizzu designated the year during his 2026 Presidential Address at the opening of the People’s Majlis.

The campaign is framed as a national, year-long invitation covering resort celebrations, local-island festivals, cultural experiences, culinary events, wellness, adventure and marine activities. The aim, the release says, is to inspire travellers to see more of the destination and to give the tourism industry a coordinated platform for 2027.

## Who spoke, and what the Maldives took to WTM

The international unveiling was a press conference titled **“Celebrate the Sunny Side – An Invitation to the World”**. Visit Maldives Corporation (VMC) chief executive and managing director Abdulla Yasir introduced the campaign, followed by remarks from Mohamed Ameen, Minister of Tourism and Civil Aviation.

A leadership panel included Hussain Afeef, chairman of the Maldives Association of Tourism Industry (MATI) and a recipient of the Order of the Distinguished Rule of Izzuddin; Minister Ameen; Abdulla Giyas Riyaz, chairperson of the VMC board; and Ibrahim Siyad Qasim, chief executive of Villa Resorts. The discussion, Visit Maldives says, covered the national tourism vision, industry participation, investment opportunities and international partnerships.

The Maldives delegation at WTM London numbered **161** representatives, including **136** participants from **62** tourism companies. The show ran from **3 to 5 November** at ExCeL London. Another highlight named in the release is the launch of the **2027 edition of the Visit Maldives Destination Guide**, continuing an annual WTM tradition.

Minister Ameen called Visit Maldives Year 2027 “a shared national journey” to showcase identity, culture, hospitality and diverse experiences while creating opportunities for businesses and communities. Yasir said unveiling the campaign at WTM London was meant to raise global visibility and drive sustainable arrivals in 2027, and that industry partnerships would be vital.

## The arrival figures in the release

According to statistics published by the Ministry of Tourism and Civil Aviation and cited by Visit Maldives, the Maldives welcomed **1,762,751** tourists from January to October 2026. **Europe** accounted for **56.9%** of arrivals. **China** was the largest individual source market, with **331,817** tourists, followed by **Russia** with **256,822** and the **United Kingdom** with **135,897**. The release notes that the UK is the Maldives’ third-largest source market, which is why WTM London is being used to strengthen European and other international trade ties.

## What we could not confirm

The official release does not give an India arrival figure, India’s rank among source markets, or any India-specific packages, campaign dates or visa notes. It also does not list airline routes from Indian cities or name participating Indian tour operators. Those details should not be filled in from secondary reports.

## Why it matters for Indian travellers and agents

The Maldives remains a core wedding, honeymoon and luxury holiday product for Indian agents, even though this particular unveiling was aimed at the London trade audience and does not break out India. Agents planning 2027 brochures can treat **Visit Maldives Year 2027 / Celebrate the Sunny Side** as the destination’s official year-long theme, and can look to the new Destination Guide and to VMC trade contacts for usable content — without inventing India-market quotas or offers that the release does not contain.
