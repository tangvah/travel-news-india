*Based on Government of Dubai Media Office releases dated 1 and 5 November 2026. Checked on 6 November 2026 (IST). This updates [our earlier note on the 1 November announcement](/travel-news-india/news/article-51-dubai-tourist-visa-visit-dubai-access-club-november-2026/).*

Dubai has [published more detail](https://mediaoffice.ae/en/news/2026/novemebr/05-11/dubai-access-club) on **Dubai Access Club**, the invitation-only tourism membership programme announced by Sheikh Hamdan bin Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum on 1 November. The General Directorate for Identity and Foreigners Affairs – Dubai (**GDRFA Dubai**) and the Dubai Department of Economy and Tourism (**DET**) are delivering it.

## What the 5 November release adds

- **Membership term:** exclusive **five-year** membership for repeat visitors, **by invitation only** through partner nominations.
- **Visa:** a **five-year multiple-entry tourist visa** is tied to the programme.
- **Activation:** invited travellers activate membership once their visa applications are approved and they meet the stated eligibility criteria; confirmed members then get partner benefits.
- **Launch partners named:** invitations will go through select loyalty programmes and multinational companies linked to **Emirates**, **Accor** and **Visa**, with more partners to join later.
- **Named benefits so far:** GDRFA Dubai's **ALSAADA Tourist Card** (privileges and promotions across the city) and a **Majid Al Futtaim gift card** loaded with credit for its retail stores and destinations. The Media Office says further offers — including hotel room upgrades, lounge access and advance booking windows for events — will be announced.

Adnan Kazim, Emirates' Deputy President and Chief Commercial Officer, said some of the airline's most loyal **Skywards** members will be rewarded through the club. Visa's statement points to benefits for **Visa Infinite** cardholders visiting Dubai.

## What is still not public

The official releases still do not list a membership fee, full eligibility rules, processing times, or an exact go-live date for Visit Dubai tourist-visa applications (also announced for November 2026 on 1 November). Do not quote fees or quotas from secondary reports until Dubai publishes them.

## What it means for Indian travellers and agents

- Frequent Dubai visitors — business travellers, families and repeat holidaymakers — are the target. Watch Emirates Skywards, Accor and Visa Infinite channels for invitations; the programme is **not** open for self-application.
- Until Visit Dubai's visa service is live, keep using current GDRFA / authorised channels. Prefer official domains (visitdubai.com, mediaoffice.ae) over look-alike sites.
- Agents should treat this as a **loyalty and repeat-visit** product, not a mass visa shortcut.
