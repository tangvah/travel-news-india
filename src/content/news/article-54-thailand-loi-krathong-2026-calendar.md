*Based on a Tourism Authority of Thailand (TAT) Newsroom release dated 5 November 2026. Checked on 6 November 2026 (IST).*

Thailand's [Loi Krathong 2026](https://www.tatnews.org/2026/11/loi-krathong-2026-lights-up-thailands-rivers-and-historic-cities/) celebrations run from **15 to 29 November**, according to the Tourism Authority of Thailand. The festival is observed on the full moon of the twelfth Thai lunar month; TAT says that full moon falls on **24 November** this year, with local programmes on different dates across the country.

Loi Krathong centres on floating decorated *krathongs* as a mark of gratitude and respect for water. TAT frames the calendar under its **Festival Economy** direction and the "365 Days of Celebration" theme for year-round tourism.

## Dates travellers will ask about

Highlights from TAT's nationwide list include:

- **Sukhothai Loi Krathong, Candlelight and Fireworks Festival 2026** — 15–24 November at Sukhothai Historical Park.
- **Bangkok Loi Krathong Festival 2026** — 17–29 November along Rattanakosin waterways, with activity sites including Rama VIII Bridge, Phra Sumen Fort, Prem Prachakon Canal and Maha Kan Fort (sub-dates vary by site).
- **Phuket Traditional Tourism Festival: Loi Krathong by the Sea at Saphan Hin** — 16–25 November.
- **Chiang Mai CAD KhomLoi Sky Lanterns Festival 2026** (Yi Peng tradition) — 24–25 November at The CAD Cultural Center Lanna, Mae On District.
- **Tak Loi Krathong Sai: 1,000 Floating Krathongs Festival 2026** — 21–24 November.
- **Yi Peng Chiang Saen: Light of Faith on the Mekong 2026** — 20–24 November in Chiang Rai.

TAT also lists events in Kamphaeng Phet, Kanchanaburi, Samut Songkhram, Udon Thani, Nakhon Ratchasima, Buri Ram, Chon Buri, Phang-nga, Hat Yai and Trang. Check the [full TAT release](https://www.tatnews.org/2026/11/loi-krathong-2026-lights-up-thailands-rivers-and-historic-cities/) for venues and exact date ranges before quoting a day.

## What we could not confirm

The release does not give ticket prices, crowd limits, sky-lantern rules, hotel packages or flight schedules. Lantern and fireworks restrictions can change by province; confirm locally before selling Yi Peng products.

## Why it matters for Indian travellers and agents

November is peak India–Thailand season. Agents packaging Bangkok, Chiang Mai, Phuket or Sukhothai for mid-to-late November can use TAT's dates to set itineraries around the full-moon peak on 24 November, while warning clients that riverside and lantern sites will be busy. Indian passport holders can visit Thailand visa-free for up to 30 days under current rules; see our [visa tracker](https://tangvah.github.io/travel-news-india/visa-tracker/) for conditions.
