*Based on a Tourism Authority of Thailand (TAT) Newsroom release dated 6 November 2026. Checked on 7 November 2026 (IST).*

Thailand took **Gold** as the [Most Desirable Country for Gastronomy](https://www.tatnews.org/2026/11/tat-receives-gold-award-for-thailands-gastronomy-at-wanderlust-reader-travel-awards-2026/) at the 25th Anniversary Wanderlust Reader Travel Awards 2026 in London, the Tourism Authority of Thailand said on 6 November. TAT Governor Thapanee Kiatphaibool accepted the award. Thailand climbed from **eighth place in 2025** to first, ahead of **Italy** (Silver) and **France** (Bronze).

Wanderlust, Official Consumer Media Partner of World Travel Market 2026, said the awards drew votes from more than **250,000** readers, with over **5.5 million** votes across 23 categories. Other nominees in the gastronomy category named by TAT include Japan, Greece, Spain, South Korea, Malaysia, Turkey and Vietnam.

## How TAT frames the award

TAT links the win to regional cuisines, street food and fine dining — including Michelin-starred restaurants — and to its **“Healing is the New Luxury”** direction and **“Must Taste”** among Thailand’s 5 Must-Do Experiences. The release also ties gastronomy to the wider **Value Over Volume** policy.

The same release notes UK connectivity work under TAT’s Airline Focus strategy: Virgin Atlantic’s London–Phuket inauguration in October, and Norse Atlantic preparing London–Phuket and Manchester–Bangkok routes in December. From **1 January to 3 November 2026**, TAT reports **818,380** UK visitors to Thailand, with an average stay of **14.18** days and expenditure of about **68,720 baht** per person per trip; full-year UK arrivals are forecast above **1,041,987**.

## What we could not confirm

The release does not give India-specific culinary campaign dates, India arrival figures tied to this award, or package prices. UK-focused airline and arrival numbers should not be restated as India-market facts.

## Why it matters for Indian travellers and agents

Food is a core reason Indians book Thailand. Agents selling Bangkok, Chiang Mai, Phuket or secondary cities can use TAT’s gastronomy positioning — street food through Michelin — without inventing new visa or fare claims. Pair with November festival calendars already on our desk (Grand Diwali, Loi Krathong).
