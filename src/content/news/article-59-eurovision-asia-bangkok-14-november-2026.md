*Based on Tourism Authority of Thailand (TAT) Newsroom and official Eurovision Song Contest Asia pages. Checked on 7 November 2026 (IST).*

Bangkok will host the first [Eurovision Song Contest Asia](https://eurovisionasia.com/news/eurovision-song-contest-asia-set-for-november-2026-debut) **Grand Final on Saturday 14 November 2026** at **One Bangkok Forum**, starting at **20:30 ICT** (19:00 IST). A **Preview Show** is listed for **Friday 13 November** at the same venue and start time. The organiser says there are **no plans to cancel or postpone** the event.

TAT [announced Bangkok as host](https://www.tatnews.org/2026/04/tat-confirms-bangkok-to-host-first-ever-eurovision-song-contest-asia-2026-marking-new-era-of-global-music/) in April, framing the debut under its Value over Volume and Trusted Thailand messaging for large cultural events.

## Who is performing

Eleven countries compete in a single Grand Final. The official [running order](https://eurovisionasia.com/news/your-running-order-for-eurovision-asia-2026) (posted 29 October) opens with **Vietnam** (Tóc Tiên) and closes with **Malaysia** (Aina Abdul). Also listed: Cambodia, Laos, Mongolia, **Bhutan** (Nyendra), the Philippines, **Bangladesh** (Beatmosphere), Thailand (MANG x DAOU), the Republic of Korea (JUNHEE) and **Nepal** (Pratik Himal Rai).

## Tickets and how to watch

- **Tickets:** A limited number remain on sale via the [official tickets page](https://eurovisionasia.com/tickets/). Buy only through links from eurovisionasia.com — third-party tickets can be refused.
- **Broadcast:** The organiser says the show will be available worldwide on the **Eurovision Song Contest YouTube channel**, with free voting via the contest website.

## What we could not confirm

Exact remaining seat inventory, current ticket prices, hotel packages and any India-specific broadcast partner beyond YouTube are not stated on the pages we opened. Confirm total price and age/ID rules on the official checkout before selling packages.

## Why it matters for Indian travellers and agents

Mid-November Bangkok is already busy with Diwali and Loi Krathong calendars. Agents packaging Bangkok for **13–15 November** should flag One Bangkok / Lumphini-area demand and sell only official tickets. Indian audiences may also follow **Nepal**, **Bhutan**, **Bangladesh** and **Malaysia** entries. Indian ordinary passport holders can visit Thailand visa-free for up to 30 days for tourism, under the scheme in force since 15 September 2026; see our [visa tracker](https://tangvah.github.io/travel-news-india/visa-tracker/).
