*Based on a Tourism Authority of Thailand (TAT) press release dated 30 October 2026. Checked on 31 October 2026 (IST).*

Indian travellers heading to Thailand in November can join Diwali celebrations in three of the country's busiest tourist areas. The **Amazing Thailand Grand Diwali Festival 2026** will run seven celebrations in **Bangkok, Pattaya (Chon Buri) and Phuket from 3 to 29 November**, TAT said. **Admission to all seven is free.**

The programme is organised by the Indian Association of Thailand (IAT) and the Thai–India Trade and Tourism Association (TITTA), working with TAT, the Embassy of India in Bangkok and other partners. TAT says this is its fifth straight year of supporting the festival, which comes ahead of the 80th anniversary of Thailand–India diplomatic relations in 2027. Diwali falls on 8 November this year.

## Where and when

| Dates | Place | Event | Diya-lighting ceremony |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3–4 Nov | Central Bangrak, Bangkok | "The First Light", followed by live Bollywood music | 3 Nov, 17:00–18:00 |
| 6–8 Nov | ICONSIAM Riverpark, Bangkok | "Lotus by the River" | 8 Nov, 17:00–18:00, riverside fireworks at 18:00 |
| 7–15 Nov | Central Festival Pattaya Beach | "Nine Nights of Candlelight", with Diwali Day activities on 8 Nov | — |
| 8 Nov | Central Patong, Phuket | "A Sunday of Flavour", an Indian food festival | — |
| 13–15 Nov | Pattaya Beach Road | "Lamps Along the Shore" | 13 Nov, 18:00–19:00, on the beachfront |
| 20–24 Nov | Little India, Phahurat, Bangkok | "Threads of Faith" | 24 Nov, 17:00–18:00 |
| 26–29 Nov | Quartier Avenue, EMQUARTIER, Bangkok | "Music on the Avenue" | 29 Nov, 17:00–18:00 |

The 24 November ceremony in Phahurat falls on a full-moon night that TAT says brings together three festivals: Dev Deepavali, Loi Krathong and Gurpurab.

Across the venues, visitors can expect Indian cultural performances, Bollywood music, festive markets and Indian food. TAT says the participating malls (Central Bangrak, Central Festival Pattaya Beach, Central Festival Patong, ICONSIAM and EMQUARTIER) will run seasonal promotions and special offers through Diwali.

## Why it matters

- **For travellers:** a family trip over the Diwali holidays can now include a free Diwali event in Bangkok, Pattaya or Phuket. Times are local Thailand time (1 hour 30 minutes ahead of IST).
- **For the travel trade:** the dates line up with the start of Thailand's high season. Agents selling Diwali and late-November packages can build itineraries around the ICONSIAM fireworks on 8 November or the combined Loi Krathong night in Phahurat on 24 November.

For more details, TAT lists its contact centre number as 1672 (in Thailand).
