*Based on a Vietnam Airlines promotion page. Checked on 12 November 2026 (IST).*

[Vietnam Airlines](https://enews.vietnamairlines.com/2026/10/danang-singapore/) will launch daily non-stop flights between **Da Nang (DAD) and Singapore (SIN)** on **17 November 2026**. This is not an India route, but it matters for Indian travellers who combine Singapore and Vietnam on one trip.

## Key points

- **Frequency:** one round trip every day, on an Airbus A321, with a flight time of about three hours.
- **Timings:** flights leave Da Nang at 15:00 and Singapore at 19:45, local time.
- **Network:** the airline says its Vietnam–Singapore service rises to 42 round trips a week: Ho Chi Minh City 21, Hanoi 14 and Da Nang 7.
- **Launch offer:** the page lists a ticketing period until 30 November 2026 and a travel period of 17 November to 16 December 2026 for the member offers.

## What it means for the trade

Da Nang becomes a direct add-on to Singapore itineraries, which helps agents who build twin-country packages. Indian travellers still reach Vietnam via Hanoi, Ho Chi Minh City or the new Ahmedabad–Hanoi service.

## What we could not confirm

The page gives no fares, and we have not checked the schedule against the airline's booking engine. Confirm times and fares with Vietnam Airlines before selling.
