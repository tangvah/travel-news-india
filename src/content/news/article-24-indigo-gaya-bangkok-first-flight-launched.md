*Based on IndiGo's 24 August 2026 announcement and posts on IndiGo's official X account and by Samrat Choudhary on 26 October 2026. Checked on 27 October 2026 (IST).*

IndiGo's non-stop flights between **Gaya and Bangkok** are now running. The service was flagged off on **26 October 2026**, the start date IndiGo gave when it announced the route in August.

## What was confirmed

- Samrat Choudhary, whom IndiGo's post addresses as Bihar's Chief Minister, posted in Hindi on 26 October that he had launched IndiGo's first non-stop international flight between Gayaji and Bangkok. He said it would strengthen Bihar's international air links and support tourism and cultural exchange.
- IndiGo quoted that post the same day and thanked him, saying it was proud to support "the enduring Buddhist - cultural and spiritual bond between Bihar and Thailand".

## Route details

IndiGo's August announcement describes the service as **non-stop and bi-weekly** (two flights a week) between Gaya and Bangkok. IndiGo has not published flight times or aircraft type in the materials we checked, so confirm the schedule on IndiGo's website or through your booking system.

## Why it matters

Gaya is the gateway to Bodh Gaya, one of the most important Buddhist pilgrimage sites. A direct link makes pilgrim and heritage tours between Bihar and Thailand much simpler, with no need to connect through Delhi or Kolkata. Thai AirAsia has also announced a return to Gaya from Bangkok Don Mueang; see our routes tracker for the latest status.
