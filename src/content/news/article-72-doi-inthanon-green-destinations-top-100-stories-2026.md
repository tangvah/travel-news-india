*Checked against the TAT release on 10 November 2026 (IST).*

The Tourism Authority of Thailand (TAT) says Doi Inthanon National Park in Chiang Mai has earned a place in the **Green Destinations Top 100 Stories 2026**, per its [release of 3 November 2026](https://www.tatnews.org/2026/11/tat-supported-doi-inthanon-among-thailands-green-destinations-top-100-stories-2026/).

## Key facts from the release

- **Announcement:** made at the Green Destinations Global Conference 2026, held 27–29 October in Kranj, Slovenia.
- **Thai selections:** Doi Inthanon plus 15 other Thai locations in the Top 100 Stories. Three Thai destinations also received Bronze-level Green Destinations Awards: Ko Tao (Surat Thani), Mae Puem National Park (Phayao) and Uthai Thani Old Town.
- **Other sites named:** these include Mae Hong Son Town, Ban Chiang, Bang Rong–Pa Khlok in Phuket, Phetchaburi Old Town, Chiang Khan, Sukhothai Old Town and Nan Old Town.
- **Programme:** Doi Inthanon was a model under TAT's Sustainable Product Prototype 2026 (Chiang Mai Prototype).
- **Total:** 31 Thai entries were supported across the Top 100 Stories and Good Travel Stories Competition in 2026.

## Reader note

Indian ordinary passport holders can visit Thailand visa-free for up to 30 days for tourism, under the scheme in force since 15 September 2026 ([Royal Thai Embassy, New Delhi](https://newdelhi.thaiembassy.org/en/content/the-revision-of-thailand-s-visa-exemption-and-voa)). Chiang Mai and the northern parks suit longer, nature-led itineraries. The release gives no India-specific offers or flight information.
