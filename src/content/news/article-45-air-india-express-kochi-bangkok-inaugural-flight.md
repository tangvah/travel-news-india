*Based on a post from Air India Express's official X account on 29 October 2026. Checked on 2 November 2026 (IST).*

Air India Express has started flying between **Kochi and Bangkok**. In a [post on its official X account on 29 October](https://x.com/AirIndiaX/status/2115804947933790501), the airline said it had celebrated the **inaugural flight** of the service and called it its **first connection from Kochi to Southeast Asia**.

The airline said the route links Kerala and Thailand more closely and should help travel, tourism and cultural exchange between the two.

## What we could not confirm

The post does not give the date of the first flight, the weekly frequency, flight numbers or the aircraft type. Air India Express has not published a press release on the route that we could find. We will add these details to our [routes tracker](https://tangvah.github.io/travel-news-india/routes-tracker/) once the airline confirms them.

## Why it matters

Kochi–Bangkok joins the other new Bangkok routes from India this season, including [Visakhapatnam](https://www.tatnews.org/2026/10/tat-welcomes-air-india-express-inaugural-visakhapatnam-bangkok-flight/) (Air India Express) and [Guwahati and Gaya](https://www.goindigo.in/press-releases.html) (IndiGo). For travellers in Kerala, it is a non-stop option to Thailand, where Indian passport holders currently get [30 days visa-free for tourism](https://newdelhi.thaiembassy.org/en/content/the-revision-of-thailand-s-visa-exemption-and-voa).

## For agents

Check the Air India Express website or your booking system for the current schedule before quoting, since the airline has not published operating days.
