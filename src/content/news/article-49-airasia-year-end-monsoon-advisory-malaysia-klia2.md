*Based on an AirAsia advisory issued in Sepang on 2 November 2026. Checked on 3 November 2026 (IST).*

AirAsia has told passengers to [plan ahead for the year-end peak](https://newsroom.airasia.com/news/airasia-advises-guests-to-plan-ahead-for-year-end-travel-amid-monsoon-season). It expects higher passenger numbers, and seasonal weather may affect travel plans. The airline says **Malaysia's Northeast Monsoon is expected to run from 27 October 2026 to March 2027**. Long spells of heavy rain are likely, especially **between November and January**, which may cause flooding in some areas. Thunderstorms and bad weather may also disrupt flights.

## Key points

- **Arrival times:** reach the airport at least **three hours before international flights** and two hours before domestic flights.
- **Check-in:** AirAsia strongly encourages self check-in on the AirAsia MOVE app or airasia.com, including for group bookings. It opens 14 days before departure and closes one hour before. Use the airport kiosks to print bag tags and drop bags yourself. Counter check-in is kept for guests with reduced mobility, those travelling with an infant under 24 months, pregnant guests, senior citizens and young guests travelling alone.
- **Before leaving home:** check flight status 24 hours before departure and again before setting out. Allow extra time for the trip to the airport. For KLIA2, AirAsia recommends the Express Rail Link (ERL).
- **Contact details:** make sure the email address and mobile number in the booking are correct, because disruption alerts are sent there.
- **Cabin bags:** one piece no larger than 56 x 36 x 23 cm, plus one laptop bag, handbag or small bag. The two together must not weigh more than 7 kg.
- **Pregnant guests:** they must fill in a form at the counter. After 27 weeks, an approved doctor's medical certificate is also required.

## Why it matters for India

AirAsia flies between India and Kuala Lumpur, where its flights use KLIA2, and it is restarting its [Ahmedabad and Amritsar routes](https://newsroom.airasia.com/news/airasia-celebrates-return-of-ahmedabad-and-amritsar-flights-with-a-special-sale) this season. Winter holidays and Christmas trips fall in the wettest part of Malaysia's monsoon. Agents should allow longer connections when clients use separate tickets, remind clients that most check-in is now self-service, and make sure the client's own phone number and email are in the booking, not only the agency's. The advisory does not report any current cancellations.
