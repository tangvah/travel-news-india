*Based on SWISS and Lufthansa Group releases and the SWISS India route page, checked on 23 October 2026.*

Swiss International Air Lines (SWISS) is adding Bengaluru as its third Indian destination, after Delhi and Mumbai. It is the airline's first route to South India. SWISS's India route page says flights start on **28 October 2026**, the date the first flight from Zurich arrives in Bengaluru.

## Schedule

| Flight | Route | Departs | Arrives | Days |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LX 140 | Zurich–Bengaluru | 13:20 | 02:55 (next day) | Tue, Thu, Fri, Sat, Sun |
| LX 141 | Bengaluru–Zurich | 04:50 | 10:50 | Mon, Wed, Fri, Sat, Sun |

Times are local. SWISS describes the service as five times a week in the 2026/27 winter timetable, which runs from 25 October 2026 to 27 March 2027.

## Cabins and connections

The Lufthansa Group release lists First, Business and Economy Class on the route. It says the flights connect at Zurich to Europe and North America across the Lufthansa Group network, which has more than 70 weekly India flights this winter. Bangalore International Airport's COO said the link will allow single-ticket transfers from Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities through Bengaluru's domestic network.

## What it means for agents

South Indian clients going to Switzerland and onward to Europe now have a nonstop option from Bengaluru, without routing through Delhi, Mumbai or a Gulf hub. Tickets are sold on swiss.com and through travel agents. Indian passport holders still need a Schengen visa for Switzerland.
